# **Creative Thinking Systems**

Applied AI for Cultural and Creative Industries

---

**Creative Thinking Systems** is an applied AI research, development, and production entity focused exclusively on cultural and creative industries. We exist to ensure that AI serves creativity rather than displacing it, and that the people, artists, creatives, and institutions who create culture retain agency, ownership, and economic reward in the process.

Our mission is to enhance, incentivise, and enshrine human creativity in an era of artificial intelligence.

---

## **What We Do**

We work with cultural institutions, sovereign infrastructure programmes, arts bodies, and creative organisations navigating AI adoption, through applied research, development, and production.

---

### **01 Research & Development**

Applied research, tools, and public demonstrators that make the fast-moving AI landscape legible for the cultural sector.

* [**Dead Internet Monitor**](https://deadinternetmonitor.com/)**:** A live diagnostic tool tracking the proliferation of AI-generated content and bot activity across social platforms, measuring how "dead" the internet is becoming.

* **Copyright Intelligence:** Monitoring how AI systems crawl and train on cultural and copyrighted content, tracking crawler compliance, and developing attribution methodologies and systems.

* **Cooperative Intelligence Benchmark:** Evaluating and informing how frontier AI models cooperate with humans on creative tasks.

---

### **02 Strategy, Development & Sovereign Deployment**

AI strategy, system design, and deployment for cultural organisations, from readiness diagnostics to building sovereign infrastructure that keeps sensitive content under the organisation's own control.

* **AI Readiness for Cultural Organisations:** Diagnostic assessments, workflow design, training, and implementation, tailored to the specific constraints and opportunities of cultural institutions adopting AI. A structured entry point for organisations at any stage of AI maturity.

* **Sovereign AI Infrastructure:** Many cultural organisations hold sensitive intellectual property, collections data, and audience information that cannot be processed through third-party cloud AI services. We design and build AI solutions on open-weight models and nationally compliant infrastructure, keeping data, content, and workflows under the organisation's control. This applies equally to national institutions managing digitised collections and to sovereign cultural programmes operating across multiple venues and sites.

* **AI-Enabled Operations:** Building agentic systems that coordinate event production, venue operations, and programming, reducing operational overhead while maintaining creative and curatorial control.

---

### **03 Production & Content**

Emerging AI-native production capabilities, developed through our R\&D programme and built in partnership with cultural clients.

* **Generative Content for Public Space:** Culturally authentic generative content and music for venues, public spaces, and cultural districts, serving regional aesthetics at scale. Currently in development through applied research partnerships.

* **World Models for Culture:** Exploring the application of emerging AI world models, systems that generate coherent, navigable environments, to cultural production: immersive venue experiences, interactive heritage walkthroughs, and virtual reconstructions of historical sites and performances. This is an active development direction building on our work in sovereign AI infrastructure.
